## Citation controls

- Prefer the primary article, DOI, official working paper, or official data portal.
- Record the exact proposition supported before adding a citation to the manuscript.
- Do not convert a literature precedent into a claim that the method is valid in this sample.
- Update this ledger with page, equation, or section locators during manuscript drafting.
- Working papers are labeled as such and cannot overrule peer-reviewed identification results without later publication checks.
