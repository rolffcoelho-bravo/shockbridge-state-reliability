## Prospective resolution

The unapproved amendment fixes a single operation order and independently
selects series within expanding and rolling histories while comparing their
fits in the already registered common calendar and channel coordinates. It
binds ordered series identifiers into each scenario hash, defines the manifest
category maps and exact-duplicate operator, and makes provenance mapping
explicit.

For B2, the proposed combination averages the unique analytic classes' scalar
states and channel projectors separately within each history. It compares the
rank-one scalar projectors, the mean channel operators, and the minimum mean
anchor capture on the same three-dimensional threshold grid. For B3, the
proposed singular-value update projects the observed residual onto the
observed cells and carries the preceding low-rank iterate on unobserved cells,
instead of treating missing entries as observed zero.

The amendment also separates calibration-only negative-control analogues from
the untouched Stage-B conditions, restricts evaluation identity work to
pre-fit input hashing, requires a schema-complete lock, and registers a
60-origin immutable calibration ledger whose hash enters that lock.

## Research value and claim boundary

This stop strengthens the paper because it documents a subtle distinction:
tested numerical operators do not by themselves define a reproducible
empirical algorithm. The B3 finding also prevents an incorrect claim that the
current code already solves the registered incomplete-data convex problem.
That correction is methodologically important, but it is not evidence that
the proposed envelope is reliable or novel.

No empirical reliability result, authorization rate, comparator advantage,
high-dimensional claim, transmission effect, or monetary-policy
recommendation follows from Run 029. The four-variable negative result remains
the only executed state-selection conclusion. All wider-panel inputs remain
real, official, and freely retrievable; synthetic values remain software-test
fixtures only.
