## Interpretation boundary

No row currently supports a positive claim about state-dependent transmission, causal amplification, trading performance, or a reliable state estimator. Runs 007–010 instead provide prospective evidence of state-measurement instability, its descriptive anatomy, and its robustness to an ex-post latest official vintage. The only real-data estimates concern the state process, its support, and its measurement stability.
