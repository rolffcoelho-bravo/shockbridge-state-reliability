## Audit question

What, if anything, can Runs 007–010 contribute beyond established real-time
data analysis, factor-model instability diagnostics, forecast-breakdown methods,
and model-selection uncertainty?

## Closest literature families

### Euro-area real-time data and revisions

Croushore and Stark establish why vintage-specific data are necessary for
real-time evaluation. Giannone, Henry, Lalik and Modugno are the closest source
reference because their euro-area database directly studies revisions to HICP,
industrial production, and unemployment and documents changing euro-area
concepts. Jacobs and van Norden show why revisions need not be simple classical
measurement error or converge to an observed final truth after a fixed number of
rounds.

This literature validates Run 010's cautious design and rejects a stronger
“pure numerical revision” label. It also means that merely comparing real-time
and latest data is established, not novel.

### Real-time factors and vintage robustness

Bernanke and Boivin, Aastveit and Trovik, and Clements provide counterexamples
to any claim that revised-data factor results must differ materially from
real-time results. Depending on target, variable, horizon, and sample episode,
factor forecasts or output-gap estimates can be comparatively robust to vintage
choice. Run 010 therefore adds a sample-specific finding: the project's negative
geometry classification is robust in this controlled euro-area panel. It does
not establish a general law about factor models or revisions.

### Factor instability and subspace interpretation

Stock and Watson distinguish instability in loadings, factors, and forecasting.
Bates et al. show that large-panel principal components can tolerate meaningful
classes of loading instability. Breitung and Eickmeier provide formal loading-
break tests, while Koo, Wong and Zhong distinguish variance and loading breaks.
Davis–Kahan perturbation geometry is classical.

Consequently, principal angles, eigengaps, projector distances, rolling windows,
and loading-break concepts cannot be presented as methodological innovations.
The project's four-feature panel is outside the large-`N` asymptotic settings,
which supports its decision to report descriptive, prospectively frozen
geometry rather than formal break p-values.

### Reliability and model-selection uncertainty

Giacomini and Rossi formalize loss-based forecast breakdowns, and Hansen, Lunde
and Nason formalize model-confidence sets. These are stronger inferential tools
for their respective predictive objects than the project's diagnostic episodes
and conjunctive kill gates. Run 009 episodes must not be relabeled forecast
breakdowns, and Run 006 model rejection must not be described as a model-
confidence-set result.

The project's incremental value is instead governance and application evidence:
model screening, point-in-time measurement, geometry diagnosis, vintage
sensitivity, and outcome sealing are linked prospectively and preserved even
when the sequence yields no eligible state.

## Positioning conclusion

### Claims ruled out

- a novel factor estimator, stability statistic, bootstrap, or break test;
- a general demonstration that factor models fail under revisions;
- a pure data-revision effect;
- a statistically estimated structural break;
- a best-model or model-confidence-set conclusion;
- any state-dependent transmission or causal amplification result.

### Defensible candidate contribution

The paper may claim a transparent empirical validation sequence showing that:

1. favorable prequential density need not imply a stable, economically anchored
   state measurement;
2. that instability survives a controlled matched-period latest official
   vintage; and
3. a prospective evidence rule can correctly terminate the transmission study
   rather than converting diagnostics into a post-result model rescue.

This is a measurement-reliability and scientific-governance contribution in a
specific euro-area application, not a new econometric estimator. “Novel” remains
prohibited until a manuscript-level synthesis checks additional application-
specific monetary-policy papers and external reviewers assess the incremental
value.

## Manuscript implication

The current working title foregrounds a transmission question that the approved
evidence sequence correctly did not estimate. A higher-integrity title and
abstract should foreground measurement reliability, point-in-time state
construction, and the negative result. Changing the project title is a material
positioning decision and is left for explicit approval after Run 011 artifacts
are complete.
